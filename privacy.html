Privacy Policy

Nightfall · Last updated: 11 November 2026 · © 2026 Umut Kedik

Nightfall ("the game") is developed and published by Umut Kedik ("I", "me"), who is the data controller for the game. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: your progress stays on your phone, there is no account, and nothing is shared unless you choose to watch an optional video or turn on anonymous statistics.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (embers, unlocks, records, completed trials), your settings (sound, language, graphics) and whether you own the Supporter Pack. This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to me. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iOS), the operating system may include it in that personal backup so your progress survives a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google or Apple account and I cannot access it. Uninstalling the game or using Settings → Privacy & legal → Delete all game data erases it.

2. Data I never collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or account of any kind. The game asks for no runtime permissions.

3. Optional anonymous statistics (off by default)

If you turn on Share anonymous statistics, the game may send, at the end of a night: the game version, the interface language, the hero, battlefield and night tier you played, how long you survived, your level and kill count, and whether you used a video revive. It contains no device identifier, advertising ID or personal data. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)). You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & legal.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

During a run, a small banner appears at the bottom of the screen for about 10 seconds every 2 minutes; it never appears in menus, while paused or on the level-up screen, and Supporter Pack owners never see it. Full-screen videos are shown only when you tap a button to watch one (to rise again or double embers). Videos are provided by Google AdMob, which acts as an independent controller and may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate (country-level) location to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalise ads.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent form (UMP, an IAB TCF-certified CMP) asks for your choices. You can change them any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Ad privacy choices.
  • iOS: the game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • US state privacy laws: Google's regulated-states messages are shown where required.

See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Purchases

The Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is processed entirely by them; I never receive your card or payment details. The game only learns whether you own the pack, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

7. Retention

On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall. Anonymous statistics: kept in aggregate for at most 24 months. Advertising data: per Google's retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK Art. 11, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, withdraw consent, object to processing and complain to a supervisory authority (e.g. your local data protection authority, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority — KVKK). Because I hold no personal data on my side, most requests are fulfilled directly in the game by deleting its data. For anything else, contact me below.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are requested with settings suitable for a general audience.

10. Security

Game data lives in the operating system's sandboxed app storage. The game loads no remote code, uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all network traffic, and validates its save data on load.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the game will show the new version before you continue playing.
